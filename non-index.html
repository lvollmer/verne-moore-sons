Our Story

Thirty Years of Roofs.
One Family. One Town.

Vern Moore started this company with a simple belief: a roof should outlast the crew that put it on. Since 1992, that's been the standard at 1012 Elizabeth Street. Not a franchise, not a national chain — a family-owned shop rooted in the same DuPage County neighborhood it serves.

Over three decades, Vern Moore & Sons has completed over 68 permitted projects totaling more than $2 million in local construction — everything from full shingle replacements to siding and exterior wall finishes. The work speaks for itself.

While storm-chasing crews roll in from out of state after every hail event, Vern Moore & Sons has always been here — before the storm, during it, and long after the out-of-towners are gone.

What We Do

Roofing Done Right,
From Day One.

01
🏠
Shingle Roofing

Full roof replacements and new installations using quality asphalt composite shingles. We size the job correctly, dispose of old materials, and leave the site clean. Most single-family homes completed in 1–2 days.

02
🔧
Roof Repairs

Leaks, wind damage, missing shingles, flashing failures — we diagnose the real problem, not just the symptom. No upselling to full replacements when a repair will do. Honest assessments, fair pricing.

03
🪟
Siding

Siding replacement and installation to match or update the look of your home. We work with vinyl, fiber cement, and traditional materials. Proper installation means better insulation and lasting curb appeal.

04
🧱
Exterior Wall Finishes

From exterior trim to wall system finishes, we handle the full building envelope. Licensed in DuPage County as a Siding/Exterior Wall Finish contractor — not a roofing crew moonlighting in siding.

05
🏗
Decks & Structural Add-Ons

Recent projects include deck construction, porch work, and residential additions. As a licensed General Contractor in DuPage County, Vern Moore & Sons can handle more than just the roof line.

06
📋
Permit-Pulled Projects

73+ building permits on record. We pull permits so you're protected — no liability, no issues at resale, and no cutting corners. If a contractor won't pull a permit, walk away.
